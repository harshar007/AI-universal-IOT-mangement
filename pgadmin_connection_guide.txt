2. STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

STEP 1: Download and Install pgAdmin (If not already installed)
  1. Open your web browser and go to: https://www.pgadmin.org/download/
  2. Select "Windows" and download the latest version of pgAdmin 4.
  3. Run the installer and follow the instructions to complete the setup.

STEP 2: Launch pgAdmin 4
  1. Open pgAdmin 4 from your Windows Start Menu.
  2. If it asks you to set a "Master Password", choose a password of your choice.
     (Note: This password is just to secure pgAdmin itself on your computer).

STEP 3: Register Your Server
  1. In pgAdmin, look at the left sidebar (the "Browser" tree).
  2. Right-click on the word "Servers", then select:
     Register  -->  Server...
  3. A dialog window will open.

STEP 4: Fill in Server Details
  1. In the "General" tab:
     * Name: Enter a name of your choice, e.g. "Nexus IoT DB"
     
  2. Click on the "Connection" tab at the top of the window, and enter:
     * Host name/address:   localhost
     * Port:                5432
     * Maintenance database:postgres
     * Username:            postgres
     * Password:            prabha0312
     
  3. Toggle the "Save password?" switch to ON so you don't have to enter it every time.
  4. Click the "Save" button at the bottom of the window.

STEP 5: View Your Tables & Data
  1. In the left Sidebar, click the arrow next to "Servers" to expand it.
  2. Double-click or expand your new server (e.g. "Nexus IoT DB").
     (If prompted, enter the password: prabha0312).
  3. Expand:
     Databases  -->  postgres  -->  Schemas  -->  public  -->  Tables
  4. You will see your database tables (such as the "users" table).
  5. To view the data inside a table:
     * Right-click on the "users" table.
     * Go to: View/Edit Data  -->  All Rows.
     * A grid will open displaying the users registered in the system.

================================================================================
Note: If your database is running inside Docker, ensure your Docker containers 
are running (via `docker-compose up` or your Docker Desktop interface) so that 
port 5432 is active on localhost.
